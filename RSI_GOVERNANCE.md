# RSI governance and promotion authority

The candidate generator is not the final judge. Role separation: proposer (low trust), experimenter (sandbox), independent verifier (operator-controlled datasets and contract), risk sentinel (protected invariants), governance operator (recorded decision), live operator (explicit human approval). No silence/timeout/favorable performance counts as authorization. Existing risk service and `ImmutableShield` keep hard vetoes for every later capital-moving action.

**Levels:** 1 bounded parameters may enter offline comparison under locked contract; 2 prompt/memory, 3 strategy logic, 4 model/policy, 5 orchestration/tools, 6 architecture, 7 evaluator/verifier, 8 RSI-algorithm changes require escalating isolation and independent evidence. Levels 7–8 can never silently self-approve or evaluate themselves. Fast loop trades off verified capability vs risk/cost; slow loop improves research procedure only on separate held-out experiments, with stricter governance. Historical outcomes and failed hypotheses are evidence, not infallible facts.

**Offline decision:** reject on protected-file modifications, data/contract/provenance/cost violations, risk or execution regression, contamination, invalid/nonfinite metrics, or dominated results. Mark `insufficient_evidence` when thresholds, sample size, external holdout, instrument coverage or attestation unavailable. Only an independently attested non-dominated candidate with predeclared economically meaningful and statistically detectable cost-adjusted improvement and all hard constraints may be `eligible_for_operator_review`. This is not promotion. First milestone **never applies any configuration**, including paper config.

**Future ladder:** isolated sandbox → deterministic/regression tests → purged walk-forward/OOS → transfer and cost stress → independent verifier and risk sentinel → signed operator review → shadow → paper → explicit human approval for live-capital canary → monitored production. Each stage has immutable baseline/version, scope/time/capital cap, operational owners, KPI/rollback thresholds, auditable signed authorization and stop/kill path. Any breach freezes promotion and reverts to last verified version via a tested restore/reconciliation process; absence of functioning rollback forbids canary. Approvals are stage-specific, expire and cannot bypass runtime risk vetoes.

`IMPROVEMENT_GOVERNANCE.md` states a p<=0.01 and 5% index example; the example is not scientifically justified for all instruments/metrics and must not silently override the newly calibrated operator-signed contract. Do not trust a local JSON file or an LLM reviewer as a human approval signature. Protect logs, failed experiments and holdout use from proposer writes; external audit remains necessary for tamper resistance.

## v2 implementation mapping (2026-09-26)

- `protected_control_plane.py` — hashed allowlist of risk/shield/execution/evaluator/governance paths; drift between proposal and evaluation rejects the candidate and logs a security event.
- `engine_v2.py::PromotionLadder` + `OperatorAuthorization` — stages `proposed → evaluated → eligible_for_operator_review → operator_approved → shadow → paper → canary → production → rolled_back`; every stage beyond operator review requires an Ed25519-signed, expiring, genome+contract+stage-bound authorization. No authorization object is generated anywhere in code.
- `meta_proposals.py` — mission level-2/level-3 (search/evaluator changes) are recorded as genomes with `recursion_level` and always gate-blocked in-process (`level_requires_out_of_band_authorization`); contract `max_recursion_depth` is additionally enforced.
- Archive roles (`archive.py`) label a *research champion* only — `engine_v2.RESEARCH_CHAMPION_NOTE` records that no configuration or capital stage is authorized.
