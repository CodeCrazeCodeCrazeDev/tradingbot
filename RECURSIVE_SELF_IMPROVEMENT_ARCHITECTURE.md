# RECURSIVE_SELF_IMPROVEMENT_ARCHITECTURE.md
## Recursive Self-Improvement Architecture Specification

This document details the first-class capabilities and structural lifecycle that govern AlphaAlgo's recursive self-improvement loops.

---

## 1. Controlled Self-Improvement Lifecycle

Self-improvement is strictly forbidden from executing un-vetted code writes directly on production branches. Every change must traverse the following non-bypassable sequence:

```
[1. Market Observation]
          ↓
[2. Failure / Opportunity Detection]
          ↓
[3. Formulate Hypothesis]
          ↓
[4. Generate Improvement Proposal (Improvement Genome)]
          ↓
[5. Independent Scientific & Engineering Critique]
          ↓
[6. Adversarial Red Team Challenge]
          ↓
[7. Blue Team Security Patch & Repair]
          ↓
[8. Sandbox Experiment Execution]
          ↓
[9. Performance & Regression Benchmarking]
          ↓
[10. Out-of-Sample Calibration Validation]
          ↓
[11. Shadow Deployment (Zero PnL Impact)]
          ↓
[12. Human-guided Promotion Decision]
          ↓
[13. Versioned Live Rollout & Rollback Monitoring]
```

---

## 2. Capabilities Capable of Improvement

1.  **World Models**: Iterating transition functions and SCM causal graph densities.
2.  **Trading Policies**: Adapting risk exposure and position sizer parameters.
3.  **Market Analysis & Microstructure**: Enhancing order flow imbalance features and VPIN intervals.
4.  **Sentiment Intelligence**: Scaling news parser tokenizers and semantic weights.
5.  **Execution Intelligence**: Refining optimal Almgren-Chriss liquidation schedules.
