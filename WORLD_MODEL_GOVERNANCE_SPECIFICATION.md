# WORLD_MODEL_GOVERNANCE_SPECIFICATION.md
## Causal World Model Governance and Uncertainty Calibration

This document governs the mathematical representation, interventional planning, and uncertainty bounds of AlphaAlgo's World Model.

---

## 1. Grounded Causal Assumptions

Predictions generated by the World Model must never be treated as facts. They are probabilistic belief states calibrated against observed market outcomes:

$$\hat{y} \sim p(y | do(x), \mathcal{G})$$

where:
*   $do(x)$ represents the interventional do-calculus action (policy proposal).
*   $\mathcal{G}$ represents the dynamically discovered Structural Causal Model (SCM).

---

## 2. Invariant Tracking & Calibration

*   **Expected Calibration Error (ECE)**: Isotonic regression and Platt scaling are applied after every epoch to calibrate prediction probability confidence.
*   **Alternative Explanations**: For every high-probability forecast, the system must generate at least one counterfactual or alternative regime scenario to prevent overfitting on current trend correlations.
