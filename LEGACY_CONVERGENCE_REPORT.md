## Classification policy

- `canonical`: one approved authority in the modular-monolith graph.
- `adapter`: retained legacy implementation awaiting port delegation.
- `research_only`: offline/replay capability only.
- `compatibility_facade`: old API/import surface with one-wave deprecation status.
- `quarantine`: not eligible for runtime registration until evidence and safety
  gaps are resolved.
- `archive_candidate`: recorded for future approved cleanup; not moved here.

constructs `ModularMonolithRuntime` directly and analysis-mode synthetic smoke

`trading_bot/api.py` manager are now one-wave compatibility facades over
`ModularMonolithRuntime`; they no longer own independent lifecycle loops.
