## 1. Meta-Learning & Self-Scaffolding

### Finding 1.1: Meta-Orchestrator Evolvability
*   **Problem:** The MetaOrchestrator has an evolve_strategies method, but it is currently a pass-through.
*   **Frontier:** Enable the Meta-Orchestrator to **generate new Workflow Graphs** autonomously. Instead of hardcoded "Standard Analysis," it should use the PolicyNetwork to propose sequences of agent roles and tools tailored to specific task types.
*   **Implementation:** Genetic Programming over Graph structures (DAGs) representing workflows.

### Finding 1.2: Cross-Domain Hypothesis Fusion
*   **Problem:** The MultidimensionalIntelligenceLayer generates hypotheses per domain (Biology, Physics, etc.) but does not yet perform **cross-domain synthesis**.
*   **Frontier:** A "Consilience Engine" that takes validated insights from multiple domains and combines them into "Unified Theory" trading models (e.g., combining a biological "mutation" strategy with a physics-based "momentum" model).
*   **Implementation:** LLM-driven synthesis of mathematical representations from the Knowledge Graph.

## 2. Swarm Intelligence & Evolution

### Finding 2.1: Expert Specialization Loop
*   **Problem:** Swarm Experts (MarketScientist, QuantAnalyst) are static.
*   **Frontier:** Autonomous Expert Hyper-specialization. If the AgentPerformanceMemory shows an agent is performing well in "High Volatility" but poor in "Low Volatility," the EvolutionLayer should trigger a **model fine-tuning** specific to that regime.
*   **Implementation:** Transfer Learning where a base transformer is specialized into "Regime-Specific Experts."

### Finding 2.2: Competitive Signal Markets
*   **Problem:** Swarm consensus is a weighted average.
*   **Frontier:** A "Prediction Market" within the swarm. Agents "bet" their performance score on their predictions. This forces agents to be more calibrated and reduces "herd mentality" in consensus.
*   **Implementation:** Internal virtual currency (AlphaCredits) used for weighting signal importance.

## 3. Algorithmic Realism

### Finding 3.1: Latent Space Counterfactuals
*   **Problem:** The WorldModel can imagine trajectories, but it doesn't perform **Counterfactual Regret Minimization (CFR)**.
*   **Frontier:** Integrate CounterfactualEngine with the SelfPlayLoop. The system should ask: "If I had NOT taken that buy order when volatility spiked, what would my Sharpe ratio be now?" and learn from the regret.
*   **Implementation:** CFR algorithms adapted for continuous latent state spaces.

## 4. Next-Gen Research

### Finding 4.1: Symbolic Alpha Gene Splicing
*   **Problem:** Symbolic equations are discovered in isolation.
*   **Frontier:** "Gene Splicing" for Alpha equations. Taking the "Volume-Z-Score" gene from one successful equation and splicing it with the "RSI-Divergence" gene from another.
*   **Implementation:** Cross-over operators in the Symbolic Regression GP loop.
