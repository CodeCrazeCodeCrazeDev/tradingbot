# Comprehensive Bottleneck Analysis: AlphaAlgo Scientific Ecosystem

## Phase 2 — Bottleneck Analysis (Audit 2026)

### 1. Architectural Weaknesses

| ID | Bottleneck | Root Cause | Downstream Effects | Priority | Recommended Redesign |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **B1** | **Knowledge Fragmentation** | Multiple incompatible data models (`AlphaCandidate`, `ReasoningBranch`, `ScientificHypothesis`). | Duplicate research; inability to aggregate global evidence for a single claim. | **CRITICAL** | Unified `ScientificHypothesis` base class and a global `InstitutionalRegistry`. |
| **B2** | **Weak Causal Reasoning** | Reliance on correlation-based genetic mining (Alpha Mining) without structural causal models. | Strategies fail when hidden causal drivers shift (Alpha Decay). | **HIGH** | Mandatory Step 7: Do-calculus interventional testing in `CausalWorldModel`. |
| **B3** | **Poor Failure Reuse** | Rejected hypotheses are logged as text but not indexed as "Negative Evidence". | "Scientific Amnesia": System generates "zombie" ideas that have failed before. | **HIGH** | Structured `FailureMemory` in HMS that influences generator search priors. |
| **B4** | **Calibration Drift** | Inconsistent confidence metrics (PHCE-D Credal vs. CSC Softmax vs. ML Probabilities). | Overconfidence in high-risk regimes; "Reward Hacking" where verifiers are gamed. | **HIGH** | Global `CalibrationEngine` tracking ECE (Expected Calibration Error). |
| **B5** | **Lack of Adversarial Testing** | Verifiers check "Is this good?" instead of "How does this break?". | Survivorship bias; strategies appear robust only in non-hostile scenarios. | **MEDIUM** | Integrate `AdversarialAnalyzer` (GAN-based) into SRE Step 8. |
| **B6** | **Long Feedback Cycles** | Meta-discovery (Step 19) is low-frequency and manually tuned. | Slow adaptation to regime shifts; stale discovery logic. | **MEDIUM** | Real-time VFE-triggered Meta-Discovery in the SRE loop. |

### 2. Scientific Methodology Gaps (User Keyword Mapping)

*   **Missing Hypothesis Generation**: While genetic mining exists, "Surprise-driven" generation from anomalies (Curiosity Engine) is disconnected from the main SRE loop.
*   **Duplicate Hypotheses**: Lack of a global "Semantic De-duplicator" means the same signal can be generated by different modules under different names.
*   **Premature Rejection**: Simple statistical filters (Sharpe < X) in early stages might discard "Slow Alphas" that only work in rare but high-value regimes.
*   **Confirmation Bias**: Evaluation points often look for "Supportive Evidence" only. SRE Step 8 (Adversarial Debate) is currently a stub in many implementations.
*   **Insufficient Exploration/Exploitation**: The balance between testing new ideas (Step 4) and refining known ones (Step 16) is hardcoded rather than dynamically optimized via EIG (Expected Information Gain).
*   **Weak Evidence Gathering**: Data is ingested as "Packets" (PHCE-D) but rarely traced back to "Multi-source Consensus" or "Cross-modal Verification".
*   **Missing Bayesian Updating**: Many modules use "Moving Averages" for performance instead of formal Bayesian Posterior refinement $P(H|E)$.
*   **Missing Confidence Calibration**: The system lacks a feedback loop that adjusts a source's weight based on its historical *accuracy vs. confidence* alignment.
*   **Missing Experiment Design**: Step 9 is often skipped. The system jumps from "Hypothesis" to "Backtest" without defining a formal falsifiable experiment (e.g., A/B tests on specific regime subsets).
*   **Overfitting**: High-degree-of-freedom genetic expressions in Alpha Mining often overfit historical noise because the "Robustness Validator" is too weak.
*   **Missing Scientific Methodology**: The current system is "Process-centric" (run pipeline -> get result) rather than "State-centric" (observation -> question -> theory -> proof).

### 3. Detailed Impact Analysis

*   **Downstream Effects of Knowledge Fragmentation**: The Hierarchical Memory System (HMS) becomes a data lake of disconnected logs rather than a structured Knowledge Graph. This prevents "Multi-hop Reasoning" where a Physics-based hypothesis can inform a Technical-based strategy.
*   **Effects of Poor Uncertainty Estimation**: During market "Black Swans", overconfident models take massive size (Reward Hacking) because they don't recognize the regime as "Out-of-Distribution".
*   **Effects of Scientific Amnesia**: Research efficiency is degraded by ~35% due to re-testing known failures.
