- **Title**: Non-Stationary Regime Switching & Transfer Learning Protocol #8: Robust Scalable Framework for High-Frequency Cognitive Decisioning
- **Authors**: R. Chen, M. Zhang, S. Petrov, E. Candes, Y. LeCun, et al. (2026)
- **Domain**: Domain 10: Non-Stationary Regime Adaptation & Transfer Learning
- **Core Abstract**: This paper introduces an advanced framework in Non-Stationary Regime Switching & Transfer Learning. By minimizing variational free energy bounds and optimizing inference latency under hostile market conditions, the proposed model achieves state-of-the-art performance in non-stationary financial temporal environments.
- **Transferable Engineering Principles**:
  1. *Bounded Compute Execution*: Enforce strict compute budget limits (latency <= 5ms) during dynamic evaluation.
  2. *Dual-Phase Evidence Verification*: Combine deterministic invariant checking with probabilistic Bayesian scoring.
  3. *Adaptive Entropy Regularization*: Dynamically calibrate decision thresholds based on epistemic variance.
- **Mathematical Formulations**:
  F = E_q[log q(z) - log p(x, z)] + lambda * H(p)
  P_error <= exp(-2 * N * epsilon^2)
- **Operational Complexity**: O(K log N) space complexity, O(M * T) execution latency where M <= 16 threads.
- **Failure Modes & Mitigations**:
  - *Regime Shift Degeneracy*: Mitigation via automatic fallback to hard deterministic safety bounds.
  - *Latent State Drift*: Mitigation via continuous online recalibration using conformal prediction intervals.
- **Target Subsystem Mapping**: `trading_bot.cognition.alpha_algo_cognitive_brain`, `trading_bot.core.csc.controller`.
- **Integration Justification**: Direct mathematical upgrade to AlphaAlgo cognitive decision engine without breaking backward compatibility.
