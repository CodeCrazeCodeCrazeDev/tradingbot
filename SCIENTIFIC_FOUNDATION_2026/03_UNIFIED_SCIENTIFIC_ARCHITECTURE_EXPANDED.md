## 2. Contradiction Resolution & Synthesis Principles

1. **Safety Primacy**: When trade proposal signals from CSC conflict with HASP guardrails or Immutable Shield limits, HASP pre-empts execution and overrides action to `HOLD`.
2. **Epistemic Falsification**: When epistemic uncertainty $\sigma_{epi}^2 > 0.35$ (EKSFT), AutoResearchClaw forces a strategy pivot regardless of backtest Sharpe ratio.
3. **Monotonic Evolution**: Candidate code from NanoResearch evolvers must pass both out-of-sample backtest gates and AST security sandboxing in EvolutionGate before deployment.
4. **Single Strategic Authority**: CognitiveSystemController maintains exclusive control over strategic active inference; no secondary orchestrators exist.
