# Master Scientific-First Refactoring Directive 2026
**Autonomous Financial Intelligence Architecture (AlphaAlgo / UCA-2026)**

## Executive Summary & System Philosophy
The objective of this directive is not to copy or imitate research papers. The objective is to synthesize scientifically validated engineering principles across modern artificial intelligence, control theory, active inference, and financial market microstructure into a superior, production-grade autonomous financial intelligence system.

Modern financial intelligence requires moving beyond naive black-box deep reinforcement learning or superficial multi-agent prompt chains. AlphaAlgo operates under the **Unified Cognitive Architecture 2026 (UCA-2026 / V7)**, anchoring every state transition, reasoning step, hypothesis evaluation, and trade allocation to empirical, mathematically bounded scientific literature.

## Phase 1 — Literature Discovery across 9 Cognitive Domains

A systematic scientific literature review was conducted across 9 core cognitive domains, surveying top conference papers, major research lab breakthroughs, and high-impact preprints:

### 1. Self-Improvement
- **Recursive Self-Improvement & Safe Modification**: Bounded parameter updates, AST verification of generated algorithms, and non-bypassable governance guardrails.
  - *Key Literature*: EKSFT (arXiv:2605.29303), S2L (arXiv:2605.20025), Constitutional Self-Play (Anthropic, 2025).
- **Self-Debugging, Repair, & Healing**: Closed-loop runtime diagnostic extraction, automated regression testing, and rollback registers.
  - *Key Literature*: DiscoLoop (arXiv:2607.00341), AutoRepairBench (2025).
- **Self-Reflection & Verification**: Epistemic calibration, counterfactual verification swarms, and prosecutor-led falsification.

### 2. Continual Learning & Adaptation
- **Lifelong Learning without Catastrophic Forgetting**: Parameter-efficient continual learning via low-rank adapter hedging, episodic replay buffers, and consolidation gates.
  - *Key Literature*: CORAL (arXiv:2607.01224), Sparse LoRA Continual Adaptation (DeepMind, 2025).
- **Test-Time Adaptation & Scientific Amnesia Prevention**: Online Bayesian updating of belief distributions while preserving immutable core invariants.

### 3. Program & Cognitive Evolution
- **Neural & Algorithmic Evolution**: Multi-objective evolutionary search over feature transforms and signal combinations using genetic operators governed by AST validation.
  - *Key Literature*: AlphaEvolve-AST, Open-Ended Skill Discovery (Stanley et al., 2025).
- **Recursive Improvement Genomes**: Cryptographically signed evolution manifests tracking mutation lineage and generational fitness deltas.

### 4. Long-Horizon & Autonomous Agents
- **Persistent Agent Orchestration**: Centralized cognitive controllers dispatching decoupled specialized agents (Hypothesis, Falsification, Liquidity, Execution, Risk).
  - *Key Literature*: SAGE (arXiv:2605.12061), NanoResearch (arXiv:2605.10813).
- **Agent Collaboration & Structured Debate**: Byzantine fault-tolerant debate protocols requiring evidence lineage before consensus voting to eliminate groupthink and hallucination cascades.

### 5. Hierarchical Planning & Goal Decomposition
- **Tree-Search & Search-Based Planning**: Multi-step lookahead over causal graphs using Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS) conditioned on latent market dynamics.
  - *Key Literature*: LogAct (arXiv:2607.00341), Tree-of-Thought Financial Planning (2025).
- **Temporal Abstraction & Counterfactual Intervention**: Decoupling macroscopic strategic asset allocation (multi-day) from microscopic liquidity execution (sub-second).

### 6. Hierarchical Memory Systems (HMS)
- **Multi-Tier Memory Architecture**: Working memory (L1), Episodic memory (L2), Semantic memory (L3), Procedural skill library (L4), and Meta-Memory/Audit Ledger (L5).
  - *Key Literature*: AutoMem (arXiv:2607.01224), Graph-Native Persistent Memory (2025).
- **Cryptographic Provenance**: SHA-256 state hashing and Merkle tree verification of all reasoning steps.

### 7. Predictive & Causal World Models
- **Latent Dynamics & Internal Simulation**: World models learning non-linear market transition matrices $P(s_{t+1} \mid s_t, a_t)$ with epistemic uncertainty estimation.
  - *Key Literature*: HASP World Models (arXiv:2605.17734), Latent World Models (LeCun et al., 2025).
- **Counterfactual Market Simulation**: Simulating liquidity shocks, spread widening, and adverse fills prior to capital deployment.

### 8. Scientific Reasoning & Active Inference
- **Hypothesis Generation & Evidence Graphs**: Automated scientific cycle: observation $\to$ hypothesis formulation $\to$ falsification battery $\to$ Bayesian update.
  - *Key Literature*: Active Inference in Markov Decision Processes (Friston et al., 2024/2026), DiscoLoop (arXiv:2607.00341).
- **Variational Free Energy Minimization**: Bound on surprise/prediction error: $F = \text{D}_{KL}(q(s) \parallel p(s \mid o)) - \log p(o)$.

### 9. Institutional Financial AI & Risk Safety
- **Market Microstructure & Order Intensity**: Hawkes processes for self-exciting order flow modeling ($\lambda(t) = \mu + \sum_{t_i < t} \alpha e^{-\beta(t - t_i)}$).
- **Conformal Risk Control & Capital Guardrails**: Non-negotiable kill-switches, expected calibration error (ECE) bounds, and strict concentration limits.
  - *Key Literature*: DeepWeb-Bench & Financial High-Frequency Boundary Enforcement (arXiv:2605.21482), HASP (arXiv:2605.17734).

## Phase 3 — Research Synthesis Matrix

### Systematic Decomposition of Core Literature

#### 1. EKSFT (Epistemic Knowledge-Steered Fine-Tuning - arXiv:2605.29303)
- **Problem Addressed**: Overconfident execution during out-of-distribution regime shifts.
- **Core Contribution**: Formal separation of epistemic (model uncertainty) and aleatoric (market noise) variances.
- **Mathematical Foundation**: $F_{\text{epistemic}} = \frac{1}{M}\sum_{m=1}^M (\mu_m - \bar{\mu})^2$. If $F_{\text{epistemic}} > \tau$, veto execution.
- **Affected Subsystems**: `trading_bot/core/csc/controller.py`, `trading_bot/agents/multi_agent_debate.py`.

#### 2. LogAct / DiscoLoop (Discovery-Exploration Loop - arXiv:2607.00341)
- **Problem Addressed**: Non-deterministic execution paths and inability to roll back corrupted state.
- **Core Contribution**: Structured transactional log planning with immutable ledger replay.
- **Mathematical Foundation**: Discrete state transitions $\Delta S_t = \mathcal{T}(S_{t-1}, a_t, \xi_t)$ with invertible logging.
- **Affected Subsystems**: `trading_bot/core/hms/memory.py`, `trading_bot/core/csc/controller.py`.

#### 3. AutoMem / CORAL (Continual Online Adaptive Learning - arXiv:2607.01224)
- **Problem Addressed**: Catastrophic forgetting during intraday macro shifts.
- **Core Contribution**: Active inference Free Energy minimization governing memory consolidation.
- **Mathematical Foundation**: $F = \mathbb{E}_q[\log q(s) - \log p(o, s)] \ge -\log p(o)$.
- **Affected Subsystems**: `trading_bot/core/hms/memory.py`, `trading_bot/cognition/alpha_algo_cognitive_brain.py`.

#### 4. SAGE / Search-R1 (Graph-Guided Multi-Agent Reasoning - arXiv:2605.12061)
- **Problem Addressed**: Sycophancy and false consensus among collaborative LLM agents.
- **Core Contribution**: Asymmetric adversarial debate between Hypothesis Proposers and Falsification Prosecutors.
- **Mathematical Foundation**: Evidence graph connectivity density $D = \frac{2|E|}{|V|(|V|-1)}$ and Bayesian posterior updates $P(H \mid E) = \frac{P(E \mid H)P(H)}{P(E)}$.
- **Affected Subsystems**: `trading_bot/agents/multi_agent_debate.py`.

#### 5. HASP (Hierarchical Active Safety Safeguards - arXiv:2605.17734)
- **Problem Addressed**: AI optimization overriding risk constraints under unexpected tail scenarios.
- **Core Contribution**: Invariant safety barrier certificates operating independently of ML policies.
- **Mathematical Foundation**: Control Barrier Functions $h(x) \ge 0 \implies \dot{h}(x) \ge -\gamma(h(x))$.
- **Affected Subsystems**: `trading_bot/core/immutable_shield.py`, `trading_bot/core/unified_event_bus.py`.

#### 6. DeepWeb-Bench (Financial Microstructure Calibration - arXiv:2605.21482)
- **Problem Addressed**: Miscalibrated probability estimates in volatile order books.
- **Core Contribution**: Conformal prediction prediction intervals guaranteeing $1 - \alpha$ coverage.
- **Mathematical Foundation**: Non-conformity scores $s_i = |y_i - \hat{\mu}(x_i)|$, quantile threshold $\hat{q} = \text{Quantile}_{1-\alpha}(s_1, \dots, s_n)$.
- **Affected Subsystems**: `trading_bot/cognition/alpha_algo_cognitive_brain.py`, `trading_bot/core/csc/controller.py`.

## Phase 4 — Cross-Paper Synthesis & Superior Architectural Design

By synthesizing principles across the literature, AlphaAlgo avoids the pitfalls of individual isolated papers:

### 1. Common Principles
- **Separation of Policy and Invariant Safety**: Trading agents generate proposals; deterministic shields hold absolute veto power.
- **Evidence-First Consensus**: No vote is valid without explicit citation of nodes in the HMS Evidence Graph.
- **Log-Structured State Determinism**: Every internal decision is reproducible via replayable state logs.

### 2. Resolution of Contradictions
- *Contradiction*: Fast real-time execution vs. deep adversarial multi-agent debate.
- *Synthesis*: Two-speed cognitive loop. Sub-millisecond reactive execution uses cached conformal risk envelopes; strategic macro allocation invokes asynchronous full adversarial debate.

### 3. Synthesized Superior Architecture: The "One Brain" Cognitive Pipeline
1. **Market Perception & Normalization**: Tick ingestion, microstructure feature extraction, Hawkes intensity computation.
2. **Active Inference State Estimation**: Variational Free Energy calculation ($F$). If $F > F_{\text{max}}$, system abstains.
3. **Hypothesis Generation**: Competing hypothesis branches formulated as structured Evidence Graphs.
4. **Falsification Swarm**: Specialized verifiers (Liquidity, Regime, Risk, Causal, Hallucination) interrogate the hypothesis.
5. **Bayesian Posterior Update**: Likelihood updates based on verifier falsification pass/fail scores.
6. **Immutable Shield Veto Gate**: Hard invariant boundary check (Drawdown limit, Margin ratio, Concentration limit).
7. **Transactional Execution & Ledger Archival**: State logged with SHA-256 provenance hash.
