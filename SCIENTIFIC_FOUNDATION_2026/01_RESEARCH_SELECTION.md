# Phase 1 & 2: Literature Selection and Quality Filtering (2026 Edition)

## Overview
This document details the literature discovery and rigorous peer-review filtering protocol for AlphaAlgo's autonomous financial intelligence platform.

## Quality Filter Criteria
Every paper evaluated was subjected to the 7-Point Quality Gate:
1. **Scientific Novelty**: Represents a fundamental advancement in cognitive modeling, active inference, or multi-agent debate.
2. **Engineering Value**: Provides direct, actionable algorithmic mechanisms.
3. **Reproducibility**: Includes verifiable mathematical proofs, pseudocode, or open-source benchmarks.
4. **Mathematical Rigor**: Formalized objective functions, convergence guarantees, or statistical bounds.
5. **Implementation Quality**: Low algorithmic overhead and deterministic error propagation.
6. **Scalability**: Sub-linear or linear time complexity with respect to horizon length $T$ and memory size $M$.
7. **Production Readiness**: Proven stability under non-stationary market conditions.
