# Phase 1: Research Discovery & Selection (2026)

This document details the selection process for the 16 highest-impact research papers that form the scientific foundation of the AlphaAlgo Unified Scientific Architecture (UCA-2026).

---

## 1. Selection Criteria

Papers were selected based on an independent evaluation of:
*   **Scientific Merit**: Peer-reviewed or high-authority industry lab status (DeepMind, OpenAI, Anthropic, Shanghai AI Lab).
*   **Engineering Impact**: Practicality of implementation within a production-grade financial system.
*   **Reproducibility**: Evidence of successful benchmarks or open-source implementations.
*   **Relevance**: Direct applicability to the 16 core domains (Long-horizon agents, World Models, Memory, etc.).

## 3. Discarded Concepts & Justification

*   **Naive Swarm Architectures**: Rejected due to evidence of "Functional Collapse" and high communication taxes (Conflict: *Effective Agents*).
*   **Pure JEPA World Models**: Moved to "Legacy" status. Replaced by CWMI (Causal Models) to handle distribution shifts and interventions.
*   **Stateless ReAct Loops**: Rejected as a primary driver. Replaced by Persistent Cognitive Agents with Epistemic Cores and Information Folding.
*   **Prompt-based SOPs**: Rejected for production-scale. Replaced by behavioral LoRAs (Skill-to-LoRA) for stability and cost.
