# Repository-Wide Scientific Audit & Redesign Specification: AlphaAlgo Hypothesis Ecosystem (UCA-2026)

## Executive Summary
This document constitutes the master, institutional-grade scientific audit and architectural specification for the hypothesis ecosystem across the AlphaAlgo quantitative AI platform. Rather than restricting evaluation to explicitly named hypothesis modules, this audit analyzes every location in the codebase where hypotheses are implicitly or explicitly created, modified, evaluated, promoted, rejected, forgotten, merged, or reused under 24 taxonomy aliases across 25 production subsystems.

### 2. Comprehensive Taxonomy Mapping Across 24 Hypothesis Aliases

Every decision and prediction across the system operates as a hypothesis under one of 24 domain-specific aliases:

1. **Prediction**: Out-of-sample forward expectation generated by ML predictors and World Models.
2. **Belief**: Agent epistemic probability distribution over hidden market states.
3. **Assumption**: Operational or structural precondition required for model validity.
4. **Thesis**: High-level macro or thematic investment hypothesis.
5. **Alpha**: Mathematical signal expecting statistical edge over benchmark.
6. **Signal**: Quantified indicator output proposing directional bias.
7. **Strategy**: Executable algorithmic policy mapping market states to execution actions.
8. **Forecast**: Multi-horizon trajectory rollout from latent dynamic world models.
9. **Explanation**: Causal attribution derived from symbolic or counterfactual reasoning.
10. **Scenario**: Simulated market trajectory under specific macroeconomic interventions.
11. **Plan**: Multi-step action sequence generated by Planner Agents to exploit an edge.
12. **Expectation**: Statistical mean and variance forecasted for trade execution.
13. **Causal Model**: Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) specifying cause-and-effect market relationships.
14. **World Model State**: Latent representation of current regime and market dynamics.
15. **Latent Representation**: Encodings capturing hidden market structure in neural memory.
16. **Confidence Estimate**: Calibrated certainty score attached to agent decisions.
17. **Research Proposal**: Candidate hypothesis submitted to SRE for empirical validation.
18. **Experiment**: Backtest or paper-trading run designed to falsify a candidate thesis.
19. **Trade Idea**: Tactical proposal for immediate execution parameter dispatch.
20. **Regime Belief**: Probabilistic classification of volatility and liquidity regime.
21. **Anomaly Explanation**: Root-cause hypothesis explaining statistical drift.
22. **Policy Candidate**: Reinforcement learning parameter set undergoing evaluation.
23. **Optimization Proposal**: Portfolio or execution tuning candidate generated by ACPE.
24. **Hypothesis**: Formal SRE dataclass object undergoing 19-stage scientific testing.

## Phase 2 — Bottleneck Analysis Across 25 Evaluated Dimensions

Each bottleneck in the legacy hypothesis lifecycle has been rigorously identified, rated by priority, and mapped to root causes, downstream impacts, and redesigned solutions:

### 1. Missing Hypothesis Generation (Priority: CRITICAL)
* **Why it exists**: Reactive trigger design relying solely on fixed scheduled timers rather than dynamic anomaly surprise signals.
* **Downstream effects**: Failure to explore novel market regimes and structural breaks.
* **Redesign**: Active Inference Anomaly Detection triggering hypothesis generation whenever Variational Free Energy ($VFE$) exceeds adaptive thresholds.

### 2. Duplicate Hypotheses (Priority: HIGH)
* **Why it exists**: Decentralized agent memory leading to isolated generation without cross-subsystem deduplication.
* **Downstream effects**: Redundant compute consumption and wasted backtesting resources.
* **Redesign**: HMS SAGE Graph SHA-256 semantic deduplication before registration.

### 3. Premature Rejection (Priority: HIGH)
* **Why it exists**: Hard single-metric cutoff thresholds (e.g., rigid Sharpe ratio cuts) without noise filtering.
* **Downstream effects**: Discarding viable long-term alpha strategies during short-term market regime shifts.
* **Redesign**: Credal set interval bounds ($p_{\text{lower}}, p_{\text{upper}}$) delaying rejection until epistemic uncertainty drops below 0.15.

### 4. Confirmation Bias (Priority: HIGH)
* **Why it exists**: Generative agents sampling evidence only from historical regimes matching their initial thesis.
* **Downstream effects**: Overconfidence in brittle trading rules that fail out-of-sample.
* **Redesign**: Mandatory Adversarial Prosecutor and Falsification Gate requiring explicit search for counter-evidence.

### 5. Survivorship Bias (Priority: CRITICAL)
* **Why it exists**: Deletion of rejected hypotheses from active databases to save memory space.
* **Downstream effects**: Repeated re-inventing and re-testing of previously failed ideas.
* **Redesign**: Permanent storage of rejected hypotheses in HMS with negative trust weights to prune future search trees.

### 6. Lack of Adversarial Testing (Priority: HIGH)
* **Why it exists**: Single-agent decision pipelines lacking peer critiquing.
* **Downstream effects**: Susceptibility to price hallucinations and unrealistic execution assumptions.
* **Redesign**: VerificationSwarm peer review featuring Causal, Liquidity, Regime, and Hallucination verifiers.

### 7. Insufficient Exploration (Priority: MEDIUM)
* **Why it exists**: Greedy exploitation of existing high-performing strategies.
* **Downstream effects**: Structural alpha decay as market dynamics change.
* **Redesign**: Multi-Armed Bandit $\epsilon$-greedy exploration allocation enforced in SRE.

### 8. Insufficient Exploitation (Priority: MEDIUM)
* **Why it exists**: Excessive parameter tuning preventing policies from running to statistical significance.
* **Downstream effects**: High transaction costs and under-capitalization of proven edges.
* **Redesign**: Minimum institutionalization lock-in period for Level 4/5 strategies.

### 9. Weak Evidence Gathering (Priority: MEDIUM)
* **Why it exists**: Relying on single-source price tick inputs without cross-modal liquidity and news validation.
* **Downstream effects**: Poor signal-to-noise ratio in decision inputs.
* **Redesign**: Multi-modal evidence synthesis incorporating Leni AI trust audits and governance weights.

### 10. Poor Uncertainty Estimation (Priority: CRITICAL)
* **Why it exists**: Point estimates used for probability without modeling epistemic ambiguity.
* **Downstream effects**: Over-leveraging on low-confidence predictions.
* **Redesign**: Second-order Dirichlet probability distributions and credal interval scoring.

### 11. Missing Causal Reasoning (Priority: CRITICAL)
* **Why it exists**: Purely correlational machine learning predictors.
* **Downstream effects**: Spurious correlations breaking during regime changes.
* **Redesign**: Structural Causal Models (SCMs) enforcing invariant causal relationship verification.

### 12. Missing Counterfactual Reasoning (Priority: HIGH)
* **Why it exists**: Lack of simulation tools to evaluate alternative historical actions.
* **Downstream effects**: Inability to learn from near-misses and tail-risk events.
* **Redesign**: Pearl's $Do$-calculus intervention engine simulating counterfactual market rollouts.

### 13. Missing Bayesian Updating (Priority: HIGH)
* **Why it exists**: Static model weights or ad-hoc rule resets.
* **Downstream effects**: Volatile jumps in belief state upon receiving single new observations.
* **Redesign**: Recursive Bayesian conjugate updates with Leni trust-weighted likelihoods.

### 14. Missing Confidence Calibration (Priority: HIGH)
* **Why it exists**: Disconnect between agent confidence output and empirical accuracy.
* **Downstream effects**: Overconfident position sizing on miscalibrated trades.
* **Redesign**: Expected Calibration Error (ECE) monitoring with Platt scaling adjustments.

### 15. Missing Experiment Design (Priority: MEDIUM)
* **Why it exists**: Direct deployment of raw signals without structured falsification criteria.
* **Downstream effects**: Uncontrolled live risk exposure during strategy evaluation.
* **Redesign**: SRE Step 9 mandatory experiment specification defining clear falsification constraints.

### 16. Poor Memory Integration (Priority: HIGH)
* **Why it exists**: Siloed flat key-value caches across modules.
* **Downstream effects**: Inability to navigate historical reasoning graphs across execution sessions.
* **Redesign**: Graph-native 8-Tier Memory OS with SAGE multi-hop retrieval.

### 17. Poor Reuse of Historical Failures (Priority: HIGH)
* **Why it exists**: Failed hypothesis traces discarded or ignored.
* **Downstream effects**: Wasted compute re-evaluating known dead ends.
* **Redesign**: Active query of failure ledger prior to expanding new hypothesis search branches.

### 18. Knowledge Fragmentation (Priority: MEDIUM)
* **Why it exists**: Heterogeneous schema contracts between research and execution layers.
* **Downstream effects**: Loss of provenance metadata during deployment transfers.
* **Redesign**: Unified SRE Dataclass contract with SHA-256 immutable provenance hashing.

### 19. Hypothesis Drift (Priority: HIGH)
* **Why it exists**: Lack of real-time alpha tracking post-deployment.
* **Downstream effects**: Degrading live strategy performance without automated shutdown.
* **Redesign**: SRE Step 17 continuous drift monitoring with automated retirement gates.

### 20. Reward Hacking (Priority: HIGH)
* **Why it exists**: Over-optimizing RL agents on unconstrained reward functions.
* **Downstream effects**: Exploitation of backtest simulator anomalies that fail in live execution.
* **Redesign**: HASP safety guardrails interdicting anomalous reward exploit patterns.

### 21. Overfitting (Priority: CRITICAL)
* **Why it exists**: In-sample hyperparameter tuning over limited historical slices.
* **Downstream effects**: High backtest performance followed by live drawdown.
* **Redesign**: Rigorous out-of-sample combinatorial purged cross-validation in SRE execution steps.

### 22. Under-Exploration (Priority: MEDIUM)
* **Why it exists**: Rigid search heuristics stuck in local feature spaces.
* **Downstream effects**: Stagnant strategy discovery pipeline.
* **Redesign**: Curiosity Engine intrinsic motivation reward signals.

### 23. Local Optima (Priority: MEDIUM)
* **Why it exists**: Gradient-based optimization trapped in non-convex loss surfaces.
* **Downstream effects**: Sub-optimal parameter configurations.
* **Redesign**: Multi-agent debate diversity enforcement and genetic evolution branching.

### 24. Long Feedback Cycles (Priority: HIGH)
* **Why it exists**: Batch evaluation requiring extended historical backtests for initial feedback.
* **Downstream effects**: Slow research iteration velocity.
* **Redesign**: Fast-path World Model simulation surrogate evaluation before full backtesting.

### 25. Missing Scientific Methodology (Priority: CRITICAL)
* **Why it exists**: Ad-hoc, unstructured code paths creating trade signals without formal hypotheses.
* **Downstream effects**: Lack of auditability, repeatability, and formal scientific rigor.
* **Redesign**: Authoritative 19-stage SRE lifecycle governing all system decisions.

## Phase 3 — Scientific Redesign & Mathematical Foundations

### 1. The 19-Stage Unified Scientific Reasoning Engine (SRE) Lifecycle

All predictions, strategy proposals, and cognitive actions must execute through the canonical 19-stage SRE pipeline:

1. **Observation**: Ingest raw market, news, and order flow telemetry.
2. **Anomaly Detection**: Compute Variational Free Energy ($VFE$) against World Model expectations.
3. **Question Generation**: Formulate structured research questions addressing statistical drift.
4. **Hypothesis Generation**: Construct falsifiable causal hypotheses with explicit boundary conditions.
5. **Evidence Collection**: Gather historical context and verify via Leni AI trust audits.
6. **World Model Simulation**: Forecast macro outcomes using latent world model dynamics.
7. **Counterfactual Generation**: Execute Pearl's $Do$-calculus interventional testing.
8. **Adversarial Debate**: Run peer review via VerificationSwarm (Causal, Liquidity, Regime, Hallucination verifiers).
9. **Experiment Design**: Define strict falsification triggers (drawdown limits, Sharpe thresholds).
10. **Execution**: Run out-of-sample simulation or paper trading experiments.
11. **Evaluation**: Compute statistical significance and effect sizes.
12. **Bayesian Update**: Execute conjugate Bayesian update weighted by Leni trust score.
13. **Confidence Calibration**: Contract credal interval bounds and evaluate ECE error.
14. **Knowledge Integration**: Abstract validated patterns into semantic rules.
15. **Memory Consolidation**: Store immutable research ledger entries inside HMS.
16. **Policy Improvement**: Recommend execution policy adjustments to ACPE/CSC.
17. **Continuous Monitoring**: Track live alpha decay, leakage risk, and concept drift.
18. **Hypothesis Retirement**: Resolve hypothesis state into one of 10 deterministic terminal states.
19. **Automatic Discovery**: Meta-discovery loop analyzing systemic rejection patterns to refine search priors.

### 2. Ten Authoritative Deterministic Terminal States

Hypotheses never disappear from the system; every hypothesis terminates in exactly one of 10 immutable states:

1. **CONFIRMED**: Statistically validated edge meeting all production deployment criteria.
2. **REJECTED**: Falsified thesis permanently stored as negative search prior.
3. **INCONCLUSIVE**: Epistemic uncertainty exceeds bounds; requires additional data collection.
4. **MERGED**: Synthesized with complementary hypotheses into a consolidated model.
5. **SPLIT**: Partitioned into distinct sub-hypotheses across specific market regimes.
6. **DORMANT**: Deactivated due to current regime mismatch, eligible for reactivation.
7. **REACTIVATED**: Restored from dormant state upon regime alignment detection.
8. **DEPRECATED**: Legacy hypothesis undergoing gradual phase-out.
9. **SUPERSEDED**: Replaced by a higher-performing, causally superior hypothesis.
10. **INSTITUTIONALIZED**: Core architectural belief integrated into baseline platform operation.

### 3. Mathematical Justification

#### Active Inference & Variational Free Energy ($VFE$)
Anomalies and hypotheses are evaluated by minimizing Variational Free Energy:

$$F = \mathbb{E}_{q(\theta)}[\log q(\theta) - \log p(y, \theta)] = D_{\text{KL}}(q(\theta) \parallel p(\theta)) - \mathbb{E}_{q(\theta)}[\log p(y \mid \theta)]$$

Where $q(\theta)$ is the agent's internal belief distribution, $p(\theta)$ is the prior, and $p(y \mid \theta)$ is the likelihood of observed market telemetry $y$.

#### Governed Bayesian Updating
Posterior belief updating incorporates Leni AI structured trust scores $w_{\text{leni}}$:

$$P(H \mid E) = \frac{\left(L(E \mid H) \cdot w_{\text{leni}}\right) \cdot P(H)}{\left(L(E \mid H) \cdot w_{\text{leni}}\right) \cdot P(H) + (1 - L(E \mid H)) \cdot (1 - P(H))}$$

Where $w_{\text{leni}} \in [0.5, 2.0]$ scales according to human-in-the-loop audit verification and assumption validity.

#### Pearl's $Do$-Calculus Interventional Counterfactuals
Causal stability $S_{\text{causal}}$ is evaluated under interventional state transformations:

$$S_{\text{causal}} = 1 - \left| \mathbb{E}[Y \mid do(X = x)] - \mathbb{E}[Y \mid do(X = x')] \right|$$

## Phase 4 — Continuous Self-Improvement (SEAL Engine)

The hypothesis engine incorporates the Self-Evolutionary Adaptive Learning (SEAL) Engine, enabling it to recursively refine its own generation and evaluation parameters.

### 1. Performance Tracking Metrics
The SEAL Engine continuously tracks system-wide operational metrics:
* **Hypothesis Quality**: Mean empirical validation score of generated theses.
* **Research Efficiency**: Ratio of confirmed strategies to total compute cycles consumed.
* **Survival Rate**: Percentage of hypotheses reaching Level 3+ promotion.
* **Calibration Accuracy**: System Expected Calibration Error ($ECE < 0.05$).

### 2. Automated Meta-Adaptation Loop
When system rejection rate exceeds 60%, SEAL automatically executes parameter adjustments:
1. Raises anomaly surprise threshold ($\Delta \text{threshold} = +0.05$) to prune low-quality observations.
2. Tightens credal contraction factor to enforce stricter empirical verification.
3. Reprioritizes curiosity search weights toward historical failure boundary regions.
