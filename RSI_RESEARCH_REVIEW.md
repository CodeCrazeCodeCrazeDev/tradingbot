# RSI research review and evidence registry

Primary-source-led synthesis, checked 2026-09-25. Evidence in a game, math or language benchmark is **not** evidence of financial trading improvement. Each entry records mechanism → assumptions → evidence → failure modes → transferable principle → AlphaAlgo implication → testable hypothesis. Peer-reviewed sources are preferred; preprints are explicitly marked. No claimed 2026 paper ID in repository comments is accepted without source verification.

| Source / evidence strength | Mechanism, assumptions and evidence | Limitations, transfer, implementation and falsifiable hypothesis |

| Bailey & López de Prado, *Deflated Sharpe Ratio*, Journal of Portfolio Management 2014, [source](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2460551) — peer reviewed | Corrects selection bias across trials and non-normal returns; relies on a known candidate search universe and enough observations. | Financially relevant, not a cure for corrupted backtests. Record **all** attempted candidates and return distribution; H: purported edge vanishes after multiplicity/non-normality correction. `recursive_self_improvement/evaluation.py`. |
| Bailey et al., *Probability of Backtest Overfitting*, Journal of Computational Finance 2015, [source](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2326253) — peer reviewed | CSCV estimates ranking reversal when selecting among many backtests. Requires enough independent folds/candidates and comparable histories. | Not meaningful on one short EURUSD split; log trials and report PBO as unavailable otherwise. H: winner selected in-sample frequently ranks below median OOS. Evaluation/experiment ledger. |
| White, *A Reality Check for Data Snooping*, Econometrica 2000, [source](https://users.ssc.wisc.edu/~behansen/718/White2000.pdf); Hansen, *Superior Predictive Ability*, JBES 2005, [source](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1198/073500105000000063) — peer reviewed | Bootstrap comparison to benchmark over model searches; Hansen studentizes and reduces influence of poor alternatives. | Search-universe disclosure is essential. H: paired net-return improvement CI excludes zero under predeclared block choices. Evaluation. |
| Laroche et al., *Safe Policy Improvement with Baseline Bootstrapping*, ICML 2019, [source](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/laroche19a.html) — peer reviewed | Batch RL falls back to incumbent in data-sparse states, under its MDP/coverage assumptions. | Not a literal financial safety guarantee. H: advantage survives independently calibrated adverse spread/slippage scenarios, else abstain. Runner/cost contract. |
| Shinn et al., *Reflexion*, NeurIPS 2023, [source](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/1b44b878bb782e6954cd888628510e90-Abstract.html); Madaan et al., *Self-Refine*, NeurIPS 2023, [source](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/91edff07232fb1b55a505a9e9f6c0ff3-Abstract-Conference.html) — peer reviewed | Reflection and feedback refine agent outputs on language/coding tasks. | The same model judging its own answer risks correlated error; use reflection for **proposal generation only**. H: hypotheses become more testable without increased false promotion under independent verifier. Slow loop. |
| Romera-Paredes et al., *FunSearch*, Nature 2023, [source](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06924-6) — peer reviewed; AlphaEvolve 2025 [white paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.13131) — not peer reviewed | LLM/program evolution with an executable scorer and bounded candidate search. | Verifiability in mathematics ≠ noisy, nonstationary markets; freeze evaluator and exclude candidate write access. H: independent replay reproduces advantage, candidate cannot modify tests. Future code level only. |
| Jaderberg et al., *Population Based Training*, [preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1711.09846); Finn et al., *MAML*, ICML 2017, [source](http://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/finn17a/finn17a.pdf) | Population search adapts hyperparameter schedules; MAML adapts parameters across tasks. | More search means more multiplicity/compute and repeated holdout risk. H: candidate scoring increases while independent net-return/risk worsens under stress. Threat model/verifier. |
| Shumailov et al., *AI models collapse when trained on recursively generated data*, Nature 2024, [source](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07566-y); Kirkpatrick et al., *Overcoming catastrophic forgetting*, PNAS 2017, [source](https://www.pnas.org/doi/abs/10.1073/pnas.1611835114) — peer reviewed | Synthetic self-training may lose rare tails; sequential updates may forget prior capabilities. | Avoid assuming image/LLM results directly apply to trading. Separate real outcomes and generated labels; regression suites on past regimes. H: new model maintains prior-regime tail control. Later model-level slice. |
| Sakana et al., *The AI Scientist*, [preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.06292) — preprint | Automates proposals, experiments, writing and AI review. | Reviewer independence and environment script tampering remain concerns; use as hypothesis generator, never release authority. H: independent reviewer overturns proxy-scored proposals. Slow loop. |

## Further primary sources and testable transfer claims

- López de Prado, *Advances in Financial Machine Learning*, Wiley 2018, [publisher table of contents](https://www.wiley.com/en-ie/advances-in-financial-machine-learning-p-9781119482086) (technical book, not peer-reviewed paper): purging overlapping label intervals and post-test embargo presume known label horizons. Implementation: fixed horizon and causal timestamps in `evaluation/`; hypothesis: removing overlap reduces apparent OOS advantage. Not a substitute for independent holdout.
- Lightman et al., *Let's Verify Step by Step*, ICLR 2024, [paper](https://mlanthology.org/iclr/2024/lightman2024iclr-let/) (peer reviewed): process-level human-labeled feedback outperforms outcome supervision in a math reasoning benchmark, where intermediate steps can be judged. Limitation: noisy financial causal steps cannot be certified by the same proposing agent; application: audit hypothesis/evaluator steps, not grant capital authority. H: stepwise audit catches unsupported assertions better than scalar profit score.
- Silver et al., *A general reinforcement learning algorithm that masters chess, shogi, and Go through self-play*, Science 2018, [paper](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aar6404) (peer reviewed): self-play improves policy in a fixed-rule simulator with reliable terminal rewards. Markets are open and nonstationary, so synthetic self-play is not independent evidence. H: synthetic-only candidate gain fails actual chronological net-of-cost replay.
- Gibbs & Candès, *Adaptive Conformal Inference Under Distribution Shift*, NeurIPS 2021, [paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2021/file/0d441de75945e5acbc865406fc9a2559-Paper.pdf) (peer reviewed): online adaptation targets long-run marginal coverage under shift. Does not guarantee finite-horizon conditional coverage or profitability. H: earlier-regime calibration does not retain target coverage under market drift; evaluate chronologically with frozen final holdout.
- Harvey, Liu & Zhu, *…and the Cross-Section of Expected Returns*, Journal of Finance 2016, [manuscript](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2249314) (peer reviewed): large factor zoo inflates false discoveries and demands multiplicity-aware thresholds. Does not supply AlphaAlgo-specific numeric hurdles. H: apparent factor gain falls below corrected threshold when full trial family is included; keep failed experiments in ledger.

## Coverage and exclusions
