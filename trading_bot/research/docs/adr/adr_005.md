# ADR-005: Closed-Loop Production Feedback and Self-Correction System
====================================================================

## Status
Approved

## Context
In legacy systems, when a deployed model degrades or experiences severe slippage, the engineering response is manual, delayed, and un-tracked. There is no automated link connecting live production anomalies back to active research priorities.

## Decision
We implement a `ProductionFeedbackLoop` service inside `trading_bot/research/research_os.py`. When live monitoring detects slippage degradation, volatility spikes, or drawdown breaches, the feedback loop automatically triggers an anomaly alert and spawns a structured, high-priority post-mortem research project in the `IdeaRegistry` to repair or replace the model.

## Consequences
- Establishes a fully automated, self-healing closed-loop quantitative research lifecycle.
- Dramatically increases research velocity and response times to live market shifts.
- Ensures the system continuously learns from actual production failures.
