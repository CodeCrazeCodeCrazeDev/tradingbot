# ADR-001: Unified Research Constitution and Governance Gates
===========================================================

## Status
Approved

## Context
In legacy quantitative research configurations, research code is compiled and promoted to live trading with minimal systemic safety validation. This leads to severe issues such as look-ahead bias, ungrounded statistical p-hacking, and high-risk performance degradation when deployed live.

## Decision
We implement an immutable, non-bypassable `ResearchConstitution` class in `trading_bot/research/constitution.py` defining core scientific rules. All operations in the research lifecycle must query this constitutional layer. Any violation (e.g. data leakage, excessive complexity, non-reproducible parameters, or suspicious Sharpe ratios) immediately raises a `ConstitutionViolation` and aborts execution.

## Consequences
- Guarantees 100% compliance with institutional scientific standards before any model is deployed.
- Prevents catastrophic overfitting and selection biases at runtime.
- Standardizes verification rules across the entire platform.
