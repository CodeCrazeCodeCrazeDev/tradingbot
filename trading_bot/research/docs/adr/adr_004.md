# ADR-004: Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR) to Control Multiple Testing Inflation
========================================================================

## Status
Approved

## Context
When a researcher (or an autonomous AI model) tests thousands of candidate trading strategies, some strategies will appear highly profitable purely by random chance. Standard backtesting reports fail to adjust for this multiple-testing inflation, resulting in severe "selection bias" and live performance failures.

## Decision
We implement Bailey and Lopez de Prado's Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR) in `AdvancedStatisticalValidation` (`trading_bot/research/quant_pipeline.py`). The DSR adjusts the observed Sharpe ratio based on the number of trials run, the variance of Sharpe ratios across those trials, the sample size (number of bars), and the skewness and kurtosis of returns.

## Consequences
- Rejects strategies that appear profitable purely due to high trial count (p-hacking control).
- Establishes a highly realistic and conservative performance expectation.
- Aligns AlphaAlgo with statistical validation protocols used by premier quantitative hedge funds.
