# ADR-007: Purged and Embargoed Cross-Validation
===============================================

## Status
Approved

## Context
Standard K-Fold cross-validation assumes that data points are independent and identically distributed. However, financial time-series are highly path-dependent and contain overlapping labels (e.g., holding a position for 5 bars means labels at $t$ and $t+1$ share information). Performing standard splits results in severe look-ahead leakage and overly optimistic backtests.

## Decision
We implement strict chronological partition check rules using purging and embargoing techniques in `ResearchConstitution.assert_purged_embargoed_cv` in `trading_bot/research/constitution.py`. The constitution requires that validation intervals must be separated from training intervals by a minimum gap of purging (to remove overlapping labels) and embargoing (to account for auto-regressive memory).

## Consequences
- Eliminates any possibility of information leakage from training sets into validation partitions.
- Prevents over-fitting to temporal auto-correlations in price action.
- Matches premier quantitative research standards for cross-validation on path-dependent labels.
