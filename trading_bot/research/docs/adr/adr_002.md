# ADR-002: State-Centric Immutable Dependency Graph
==================================================

## Status
Approved

## Context
Process-centric pipelines run isolated scripts (e.g. `backtest.py`), making it difficult to trace why a specific model was created, what raw dataset was used, or what hypothesis was tested. This lack of lineage makes exact replication impossible.

## Decision
We represent the entire quantitative research platform as a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) of Immutable Research Objects. Every hypothesis, dataset, feature, model, and alpha is a first-class `ResearchObject` containing direct lineage parent IDs. All relationships are cataloged in a semantic `ResearchDependencyGraph` (using NetworkX).

## Consequences
- Enables multi-hop lineage tracing, allowing any production model to be traced back to its raw uncleaned dataset.
- Enforces strict immutability: once a research object is validated or deployed, its payload can never be modified.
- Enhances collaboration and prevents redundant effort by indexing all historical research paths.
