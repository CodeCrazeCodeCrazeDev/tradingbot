# Recursive Self-Improvement Audit (AlphaAlgo 2026)

## 1. Objectives & Scope of the Audit
This document presents a comprehensive repository-wide audit of AlphaAlgo's self-improvement mechanisms, identifying existing paths for recursive enhancement, duplicate reasoning loops, safety gate integrations, and potential risks of evaluator manipulation (reward hacking).

## 2. Inventory of Existing Self-Improvement Paths

### 2.1 Research and SRE Optimization
- **Path:** `ScientificReasoningEngine.discover_new_hypotheses()` (Step 19)
- **Mechanism:** Automatically monitors rejection rates across SRE registry. If the rejection rate exceeds $60\%$, SRE recursively adjusts its anomaly threshold, contraction rates, and base likelihood scales to filter noise.
- **Production Effect:** Adjusts SRE search strictness in real time. Can trigger `alpha_mining.adjust_search_strategy()` to alter evolutionary genome parameters.

### 2.2 SAGE Graph Evolution
- **Path:** `SAGEGraphMemory.evolve_weights()` & `SAGEGraphMemory.compact_graph()`
- **Mechanism:** Implements triplet validity and edge optimization. Adjusts edge weights based on historical downstream reward/success metrics (learning rate $\eta = 0.1$).
- **Production Effect:** Low-utility edges are automatically pruned if weights drop below $0.1$. Prevents memory bloat and optimizes multi-hop retrieval relevance.

### 2.3 AutoMem Metamemory Adjustment
- **Path:** `HierarchicalMemorySystem.seal_adapt_memory_window()`
- **Mechanism:** Adapts `memory_window_size` based on downstream performance and retrieval latency.
- **Production Effect:** Window size is contracted when retrieval latency is high (optimizing RAM lookup), or expanded when context retention yields high-quality retrieval.

### 2.4 EvolutionGate (RSEA & EKSFT)
- **Path:** `EvolutionGate.validate_evolution()`
- **Mechanism:** Compares candidate stateful models against stateless baselines using the CL-Bench Gain Metric ($G$).
- **Production Effect:** Autonomously promotes models that demonstrate a statistically significant gain while remaining within protected metric limits (latency, drawdown, safety).

## 5. Vulnerability & Risk Analysis

### 5.1 Evaluator Independence (The Modifies-Evaluator Vulnerability)
- **Risk:** A self-improvement agent modifies its own evaluation engine to lower safety thresholds or inflate performance metrics.
- **Remediation:** Strict isolation of evaluation libraries. Safety gates and immutable benchmark definitions are compiled outside of the customizable agent space, protected by cryptographic signatures.

### 5.2 Epistemic Ambiguity and Uncertainty Loss
- **Risk:** High-confidence outputs generated by deep-learning forecast models hide massive epistemic uncertainty under out-of-distribution conditions.
- **Remediation:** Mandatory Credal Interval estimation $[\underline{P}, \overline{P}]$ at Step 13. Hypotheses with $\overline{P} - \underline{P} > 0.40$ are immediately returned to Step 5 (Evidence Collection).

### 5.3 Scientific Amnesia (Erasing Failures)
- **Risk:** Deleting failed strategy genomes or negative evidence from memory, causing the system to periodically re-discover the same failing structures.
- **Remediation:** All rejected hypotheses must be written with extensive metadata to HMS Level T6/T7 memory. The hypothesis generator must query this failure memory prior to spawning new candidates.
